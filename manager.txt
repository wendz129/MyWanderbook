A person who manages a vlogger's content, uploads, and channel strategy is called a YouTube Channel Manager, while someone who handles their business deals and career is a Talent Manager or Influencer Manager.

Types of Vlogging Manager (Operations)
  Handles day-to-day work.
  Uploads videos, writes titles, and does SEO.
  Looks at viewer data and stats.
  Coordinates video editors and thumbnail designers.

Talent / Influencer Manager (Business)
   Finds and negotiates brand deals.
   Signs business contracts.
   Protects the vlogger's public image.
   Plans long-term career growth.
